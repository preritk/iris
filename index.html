What it does

Three jobs, done properly.

Everything runs on the official WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API) from Meta — no unofficial libraries, no browser automation, nothing that puts your number at risk. If you've been looking for WhatsApp Business API access in India without a monthly platform subscription, that is precisely what Iris is.

Bulk sending

Upload your opted-in contacts, pick a Meta-approved template, and send to the whole list in one pass — with per-recipient personalisation.

  • CSV import of opted-in contacts
  • Template variables per recipient
  • Delivered, read and failed status per row
  • Automatic queueing inside your messaging tier

Scheduled campaigns

Write the campaign once and let it go out at the right moment — a single future send, or a recurring cadence for a segment you've defined.

  • One-off sends at a date and time
  • Recurring weekly, fortnightly or monthly runs
  • Segment by any field on your contact list
  • Pause, edit or cancel before it fires

Custom agent workflows

Decide what happens after someone replies. Route it, answer it, qualify it, log it, or hand it to a human with the full thread attached.

  • Branch on the customer's reply
  • Follow-up templates and reminders
  • Write back to your CRM or sheet
  • Human handoff with full context
How it works

From zero to first send in four steps.

Meta bills your messages, not us

Message delivery is billed separately by Meta, directly to you. Iris onboards you under Meta's Tech Provider model: you own your WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) and attach your own payment method, so Meta's per-message charges land on your account, not ours. You pay Meta wholesale rates directly — we don't mark up your messages.

What Meta charges depends entirely on your template category, and the spread is large. Here is the arithmetic on a full 2,000-message pack at Meta's approximate India rates:

Meta's charges — approximate India rates, billed by Meta to your account
Template category Meta rate per message 2,000 messages
Marketing ~₹0.86 ~₹1,720
Utility / Authentication ~₹0.115 ~₹230
Service replies Free ₹0

That is roughly a 7.5x swing between marketing and utility templates on the same 2,000 messages — which is why we'd rather you saw the numbers before you paid us anything. Meta's rates are set by Meta and change from time to time; treat these as indicative. 18% GST applies to both Meta's charges and the Iris platform fee for customers in India.

One more thing about brand-new numbers. A freshly registered WhatsApp number starts on a limited messaging tier — typically 250 business-initiated conversations per 24 hours — and scales up as your quality rating holds and your business gets verified. So a 2,000-message pack may take a few days to work through on a brand-new number. Iris queues the remainder automatically rather than failing the send.
